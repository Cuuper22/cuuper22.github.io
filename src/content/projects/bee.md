Spelling bee word game. Hexagonal grid, 370K-word dictionary filtered through quality gates (100 retries per puzzle to guarantee solvability), 5 hint types, 80 tests across 9 test files. React Native with Expo. iOS, Android, web.

Built on a Manus platform scaffold. I wrote the game logic, they wrote the infrastructure. Felt like I should mention that.

Sometimes you just want to build something that isn't about AI alignment or tax law.
